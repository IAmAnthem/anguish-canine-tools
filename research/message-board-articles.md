Title: 'Tracking Canine Growth' // Lullaby <21> Tue Oct 17 02:21:39 2023

Since I saw Puscifer's note here, I thought I'd share
with you folks how I track canine times.
First off, it's just easier to only track the pet's short description.
1: a small (color) canine-type
2: a (color) canine-type
3: a large (color) canine-type
4: a very large (color) canine-type

At tame, I record the Generation and the Stage of pet.
Note that generation really only matters up till gen10,
but knowing that I've had 46 canines on this alt is interesting data.

So at tame I do this:
score
Note my character age
For the example of my 44th pet
nick 44-1 21.22.57

When the short description changed to second stage
nick 44-2 21.23.34

Likewise for stages 3 and 4
nick 44-3 22.9.18
oops, duno how to edit that. oh well
nick 44-3 22.0.37
nick 44-4 22.1.7

I stop tracking at very large, because they reach breeding
maturity and I make a new pet.

I only keep a couple in my nicks, just to be on the lookout
for changes in the time it takes.

Also note, you age differently than your canine when you statue.
So don't bother doing this if you're going to statue for 20 minutes, unidle,
and feed.
Your times will be inaccurate.


Title: 'FPQ V1.0 (Frequent Puppy Questions)' // Anthem <36> Wed Oct 6 17:55:49 2021

The FPQ (Frequent Puppy Questions)

There may be some people curious about the progress on efforts to breed
traited canines up. Quite a lot of time has been spent by a core group
of players. The most important takeaway I can give you is, ANYONE
CAN DO THIS. If you are willing to put in a rather significant amount
of time.

Initially, several years of random breeding with limited guidance and
information resulted in very poor results over time. Hopefully we have
determined SOME of the most critical things to know in breeding.

1) Does it matter if my pet is a dog/fox/jackal?
No, all races of canine will grow to the same size and power given
the same traits and time/effort growing them.

There is a penalty for mixed-race breeding.
Despite all canines being equivalent (fox = wolf = dog), you will
have less positive results from a mixed race combination.
Related: Coat colors, markings, eye color, gender are purely
cosmetic and have no play-effect.

2) Does it matter who I breed with?
There is a penalty for mating related pets.
The more closely related, the stronger the penalty.
USE THE WEBSITE! All rangers can login to the anguish.org
website, look at their player's stats, and scroll down to see their
canine family tree. Note the PARENT and GRANDPARENT columns.
This is all the information you need, if you keep detailed records,
you can determine who is related to who WITHOUT the need for your alt
to develop "relationships" when comparing pets. It is still really
important to develop "certain" compare results. "Think" and "feel"
are skewed and will be inaccurate in comparing pets.

You want the result of 'compare X to Y' to read like this:
You look hard at a large charcoal trained X comparing to a huge slate
grey trained Y ...
You are certain that X is:


3) Are puppy stats predictable?
There is too much randomness in the creation of puppies to make real
predictions. After a good bit of breeding you will develop a sense
of what ranges of results could come out.

Averages are a good starting point for guessing puppy results.
The RNG Gods may smile or frown on you.

Placing constraints on your desired puppy makes this challenging
(color, breed, gender).

4) What's a "good" puppy?
You need to make the decision on how much TIME you are willing
to spend to get a particular result. How many points up do you
think you should get for the time you are spending?

Fast Raise:
Each successive generation is faster to reach VL2, to a limit.
Caveats:
Must reach VL2 (Level 10)
4h is a good target for time to VL2

Fast Heat:
Each successive pregnancy may make heat time shorter.
Each missed heat may make heat time longer.

After you invest a number of hours getting the pet to fast raise/fast heat
the minimum time investment for a breeding cycle is:

45 minutes to 90 minutes: Female goes into heat afer pups tamed away
45 minutes: Puppies can be tamed away (lessa if bad, player if good)
REPEAT if undesirable puppies
4 hours: time to grow from tamed to breedable.

5) Why aren't you running around powerplaying?
Don't want to. You're not my mom. Go away.
On a more serious note, every minute spent not-breeding slows
down progress on breeding.
Why would I risk losing progress?
A dead pet means another 36 hours to recreate the
bloodline and maintain genetic separation.
We have some goals we want to hit before we raise some pets to their
max level. I can powerplay anytime with other alts.


6) What's important in a canine's traits?
Without anything except a single word describing each trait, we
really have no idea the full scope of all traits and their effects.

Some of these effects are easily visible now and we aren't even halfway
yet.

7) Does total trait points matter?
Does 1700 skills matter? You're never using more than 3 at a time...

It's just a measure of progress up a ladder.
It is a poor measure of relative potential.

Each trait influences SOMETHING.
If that thing is important to YOU, raise it.

There's a difference between buying a lottery ticket every week,
and investing in the stock market every week.
In the long run, one of these strategies is a lot more likely to pay off.

8) How much time does this take?
It is entirely possible to build a closed-loop breeding cadre with
a minimum of 3 alts per human. More genetic variation is preferable.

My records start in July 2019, since then we have raised about 365 pets,
over the course of well over 3500 hours.
That's at least 800 litters, thousands of puppies born, to get those 365.
This does not include both of us having multiple gigantic untraited pets.

It took about 80 hours to develop an excel / google sheets document
that acts like a database to make statting pets easier.

It took another 80 hours to figure out how to build a way to translate
the canine family tree into a usable tool to plan out my breedings in
advance and know the pets will always be unrelated.

I'm guessing another 160+ hours to turn all this into a relational
database, but I'm unlikely to do it, not enough juice for the squeeze.

9) Is it worth YOUR time? If you are a MORE POWER RIGHT NOW player,
raising pets isn't for you. And don't ask me, I'm not giving
away a free ride to the top.

Is it worth MY time? I play AA the way I want to play AA.
My goals are my own.

10) Give me all your data.
No.
You build your own data.
I'd be fascinated to hear to theories about how things work!

11) Are you a nerd and a cleverbot? You're on all the time.
We're an AA couple. We met here.
AA has always been part of our life.

Quite often AA is a small window while other computer work is being done.
Or picking up groceries, walking the dog, etc.

We might be slow to respond or miss your tells in dotimes feeding spam.

12) Will you breed with my pet?
Generally, no.
Exception for untraited pets, everyone deserves a good start as below.

13) Can I have a puppy?
I would highly advise starting with ANY traited pet over a forest-tame.
Forest tames do not count towards "fast raise". Traited pets do.
Forest tames would be useful for changing BREED or COLOR late
in the plan for your character.

If you might want to raise one or two to see how different they are
than your old-school canines, we are happy to give you a kickstart with
a top tier pet + a zero (npc or old school canine), and you can start
your own clan. Bonus points if you care about a fun color.

If you consistently starve pets, get them killed, etc, stop asking.
Not worth my giving up 2 hours of my time because you're not careful.

My Breeders: Ballad, Cover, Crescendo, Dirge, Exie, Lullaby, Mulapin,
Refrain, etc...
